COMING SOON: OUR LITTLE UK ADVENTURE

Seven days.
With you.

London, a little magic, Christmas together, and our own Oxford love story. These are the places I want to explore with you.

01

DAY ONE · FINALLY, YOU

A hug, a little London, and a secret mission

  • Finally see you and give you the hug I’ve been saving.
  • Drop off my bags and get something good to eat.
  • Head to Vauxhall Bridge to see the MI6 building from outside.
  • Get you to take a photo of me pretending I’m James Bond.
  • See Big Ben and Westminster Bridge, then walk along the South Bank.
  • Find somewhere cosy for dinner before getting some rest.

My first mission: get a hug from you. My second: I am literally James Bond.

02

DAY TWO · A LITTLE HOGWARTS MAGIC

You, me, and the wizarding world

  • Head to Warner Bros. Studio Tour for our Harry Potter day.
  • Explore the Great Hall and imagine getting our Hogwarts letters.
  • Walk through Diagon Alley and choose our favourite shopfront.
  • Take photos at Platform 9¾ and see the Hogwarts Express.
  • Try Butterbeer and decide whether it lives up to the hype.
  • Pick a little souvenir before heading back for dinner.

A whole day for this. I already know we’re taking too many photos.

03

DAY THREE · CHRISTMAS EVE IN LONDON

Christmas lights and stolen kisses

  • Explore Covent Garden and take Christmas photos together.
  • Walk through Seven Dials and find Neal’s Yard.
  • Head towards Carnaby Street and Regent Street for the lights.
  • Stop for something warm and pick a small treat for each other.
  • Get our Christmas food sorted before heading back early.

Holding your hand through London at Christmas sounds like something I’ve been waiting a long time to do.

04

DAY FOUR · CHRISTMAS DAY

My Christmas wish is you

  • Wake up slowly and make breakfast together.
  • Exchange presents and take our Christmas photo.
  • Cook Christmas lunch with our favourite music playing.
  • Wrap up warm for a little walk near where we’re staying.
  • Spend the evening under a blanket watching Christmas films.

No rushing anywhere. I want to enjoy spending Christmas with you.

05

DAY FIVE · BOXING DAY EXPLORING

Royal views and pretty streets

  • Start with breakfast before a walk through St James’s Park.
  • See Buckingham Palace and take our very touristy photos.
  • Head to Notting Hill to wander past the colourful houses.
  • Walk along Portobello Road and stop for photos.
  • Find dinner somewhere open, or head back for a cosy meal.

A day for exploring outside, with plenty of excuses to stop and warm up together.

06

DAY SIX · OUR OWN OXFORD STORY

Pretending we’re in My Oxford Year

  • Take an early trip to Oxford and pretend we belong there.
  • Walk through Radcliffe Square and take photos by the Radcliffe Camera.
  • Explore the streets around the Bodleian Library.
  • Find the Bridge of Sighs for another photo together.
  • Visit Christ Church if it’s open and we can get tickets.
  • Find a cosy café and discuss our very imaginary Oxford student lives.
  • Head back to London and pack for Devon.

We can pretend we’re in the movie. I’d quite like a whole Oxford chapter with you.

07

DAY SEVEN · BACK TO DEVON

Our adventure comes with us

  • Have breakfast and check that we’ve packed everything.
  • Get snacks ready for the journey back to Devon.
  • Listen to our playlist and look through our photos.
  • Choose our favourite memory from the week.
  • Get settled in Devon and spend a quiet evening together.

Leaving London, but I still get to be with you.

We don’t have to do everything. These are just a few things I’m excited to share with you.
